## Verb Forms

### Form I (فَعَلَ) - Base Form
| Pattern | Meaning | Example from ح س ن |
|---------|---------|-------------------|
| فَعَلَ | Basic action | حَسُنَ (to be good) |
| يَفْعَلُ / يَفْعُلُ / يَفْعِلُ | Present tense | يَحْسُنُ |

### Form II (فَعَّلَ) - Intensive/Causative
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| فَعَّلَ | Intensification or causation | حَسَّنَ (to beautify, make good) |
| يُفَعِّلُ | Present | يُحَسِّنُ |

### Form III (فَاعَلَ) - Reciprocal/Attempt
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| فَاعَلَ | Reciprocal action, attempting | — |
| يُفَاعِلُ | Present | — |

### Form IV (أَفْعَلَ) - Causative
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| أَفْعَلَ | Making someone do the action | أَحْسَنَ (to do good, to perfect) |
| يُفْعِلُ | Present | يُحْسِنُ |

### Form V (تَفَعَّلَ) - Reflexive of II
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| تَفَعَّلَ | Reflexive of Form II | تَحَسَّنَ (to become better) |
| يَتَفَعَّلُ | Present | يَتَحَسَّنُ |

### Form VI (تَفَاعَلَ) - Mutual/Pretense
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| تَفَاعَلَ | Mutual action or pretense | — |
| يَتَفَاعَلُ | Present | — |

### Form VII (اِنْفَعَلَ) - Passive/Reflexive
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| اِنْفَعَلَ | Passive or reflexive | — |
| يَنْفَعِلُ | Present | — |

### Form VIII (اِفْتَعَلَ) - Reflexive
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| اِفْتَعَلَ | Reflexive with effort | اِحْتَسَنَ |
| يَفْتَعِلُ | Present | يَحْتَسِنُ |

### Form IX (اِفْعَلَّ) - Colors/Defects
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| اِفْعَلَّ | Acquiring color/defect | — |
| يَفْعَلُّ | Present | — |

### Form X (اِسْتَفْعَلَ) - Seeking/Considering
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| اِسْتَفْعَلَ | Seeking or considering | اِسْتَحْسَنَ (to consider good) |
| يَسْتَفْعِلُ | Present | يَسْتَحْسِنُ |

## Common Affixes

### Prefixes
| Prefix | Usage |
|--------|-------|
| ال | Definite article (المحسن, الإحسان) |
| بِ | Preposition "with/by" |
| لِ | Preposition "for/to" |
| كَ | Preposition "like" |
| وَ | Conjunction "and" |
| فَ | Conjunction "so/then" |

### Suffixes - Plural Markers
| Suffix | Usage | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| ون / ين | Sound masculine plural | محسنون / محسنين |
| ات | Sound feminine plural | محسنات |
| Broken plurals | Various patterns | حِسَان، أَحْسَن |

### Suffixes - Pronouns
| Suffix | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ـهُ / ـهَا / ـهُم / ـهُنَّ | His/her/their |
| ـكَ / ـكِ / ـكُم / ـكُنَّ | Your (m/f/pl) |
| ـنَا | Our |
| ـي | My |

### Suffixes - Feminine Markers
| Suffix | Usage |
|--------|-------|
| ـة (taa marbuta) | Feminine singular |
| ـى (alif maqsura) | Some feminine forms |

## Search Strategy

When searching for a variation in the Quran:

1. **Base form**: Search the exact variation (e.g., محسن)
2. **With ال**: Add definite article (المحسن)
3. **Plurals**: Add plural suffixes (محسنون، محسنين، محسنات)
4. **With pronouns**: Add pronominal suffixes (محسنهم، محسنها)
5. **Verb conjugations**: All persons/genders/numbers

Example search list for محسن:
- محسن، المحسن، محسنون، المحسنون، محسنين، المحسنين، محسنات، المحسنات
- محسنة، المحسنة، محسنتين

Example search list for إحسان:
- إحسان، الإحسان، إحسانا، بإحسان، بالإحسان
