# Morphological Analysis: Root ج ر م (J-R-M)

## Root Letters
**ج ر م** (Jeem - Raa - Meem)

## Lexical Meaning (from classical sources)

### Lisan al-Arab / Al-Mufradat
- **Original meaning**: جَرَمَ = to cut, to sever (especially cutting dates from a palm tree)
- **جَرَمَ الشيءَ** = he cut the thing
- **الجَرْم** = the act of cutting
- **أَجْرَمَ** = to commit a crime/sin; to cut oneself off from good
- The connection: إجرام is metaphorically "cutting oneself off" from righteousness

## Detailed Variation Analysis

### Form I Verb: يَجْرِمُ (yajrimu)

**Pattern**: فَعَلَ - يَفْعِلُ
**Quranic form**: يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ (with emphatic nun)
**Structure**: يَجْرِمَ + نَّ + كُمْ

**Quranic Occurrences (3 total)**:

| Verse | Arabic Text | Subject (What causes إجرام) | Result (The إجرام) |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| 5:2 | وَلَا يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شَنَآنُ قَوْمٍ... أَن تَعْتَدُوا | شنآن (hatred) | تعتدوا (transgression) |
| 5:8 | وَلَا يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شَنَآنُ قَوْمٍ عَلَى أَلَّا تَعْدِلُوا | شنآن (hatred) | ألا تعدلوا (being unjust) |
| 11:89 | لَا يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شِقَاقِي أَن يُصِيبَكُم | شقاق (opposition) | يصيبكم (being struck by punishment) |

**Key insight**: The verb takes two objects:
1. The people being warned (كم)
2. The harmful result (أن + verb)

### Form IV Verb: أَجْرَمَ (ajrama)

**Pattern**: أَفْعَلَ - يُفْعِلُ
**Quranic form**: أَجْرَمُوا (past tense, plural)

**Quranic Occurrences (3 total)**:

| Verse | Context |
|-------|---------|
| 6:124 | سَيُصِيبُ الَّذِينَ أَجْرَمُوا صَغَارٌ عِندَ اللَّهِ - Humiliation will strike them |
| 30:47 | فَانتَقَمْنَا مِنَ الَّذِينَ أَجْرَمُوا - We took retribution from them |
| 83:29 | إِنَّ الَّذِينَ أَجْرَمُوا كَانُوا مِنَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا يَضْحَكُونَ - They used to mock believers |

### Form IV Verb: أَجْرَمْنَا (ajramnā) ⭐ NEW DISCOVERY

**Pattern**: أَفْعَلْنَا (1st person plural past)
**Quranic form**: أَجْرَمْنَا (we committed إجرام)

**Quranic Occurrence (1 total)**:

| Verse | Context |
|-------|---------|
| 34:25 | قُل لَّا تُسْأَلُونَ عَمَّا **أَجْرَمْنَا** وَلَا نُسْأَلُ عَمَّا تَعْمَلُونَ - Rhetorical: "You won't be asked about our crime, we won't be asked about your actions" |

**Why this was missed**: The initial search focused on 3rd person forms (أَجْرَمُوا). The 1st person plural (نا) conjugation was overlooked because:
1. It appears in prophetic speech/dialogue, not narrative
2. It's a rhetorical/hypothetical usage
3. The methodology didn't have an explicit verb conjugation checklist

### Form IV Masdar + Present Tense: إِجْرَامِي + تُجْرِمُونَ ⭐ NEW DISCOVERY

**Verse 11:35** contains TWO unique forms not found elsewhere:

| Form | Pattern | Arabic | Meaning |
|------|---------|--------|---------|
| **إِجْرَامِي** | إِفْعَال + ي | My إجرام | Verbal noun (masdar) with 1st person possessive |
| **تُجْرِمُونَ** | تُفْعِلُونَ | You commit إجرام | 2nd person plural present tense |

**Full Verse**:
> أَمْ يَقُولُونَ افْتَرَاهُ ۖ قُلْ إِنِ افْتَرَيْتُهُ فَعَلَيَّ **إِجْرَامِي** وَأَنَا بَرِيءٌ مِّمَّا **تُجْرِمُونَ**

**Significance**: This verse directly equates إجرام with افتراء (fabrication about Allah). The Prophet is commanded to say: "If I have fabricated it, then upon me is my إجرام, and I am innocent of what إجرام you commit." This confirms that fabricating lies about Allah is a core definition of إجرام.

## Pattern Meaning Analysis

| Pattern | General Meaning | Application to ج ر م |
|---------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **مُفْعِل** (Active participle of IV) | One who does the action intensively/transitively | One who actively commits/earns إجرام |
| **يَفْعِلُ** (Form I imperfect) | Causative in this context | To cause/lead someone toward إجرام |
| **أَفْعَلَ** (Form IV) | To make someone/something do X | To earn/commit إجرام upon oneself |

## Grammatical Notes

1. **مُجْرِم is always used as a noun/adjective** in the Quran, not as a true active participle. It describes a category of people, not a momentary action.

2. **The phrase لا جَرَمَ** functions idiomatically as an emphatic assertion, having lost its compositional meaning.

3. **The verb يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ** is always used with:
   - Prohibition (لا)
   - Emphatic nun (نَّ)
   - A causative subject (شنآن/شقاق)
   - A result clause (أن + verb)

4. **الْمُجْرِمِينَ vs الْمُجْرِمُونَ**: The nominative form (المجرمون) appears when they are the subject of a sentence (e.g., يقسم المجرمون). The accusative/genitive form (المجرمين) appears as object or after prepositions (e.g., نجزي المجرمين).
