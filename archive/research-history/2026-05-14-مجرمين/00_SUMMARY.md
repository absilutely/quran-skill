# Research Summary: المجرمين / المجرمون (Al-Mujrimeen)

## Research Question
Who are the people described as المجرمين (mujrimeen) in the Quran, and what actions/characteristics define them?

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Root Analysis
**Root**: ج ر م (J-R-M)
**Original meaning**: To cut, sever, harvest (cutting dates from a palm tree)
**Derived meaning**: To cut oneself off from righteousness; to commit what severs one from good

### 3. Who Are the Mujrimeen? (Defining Characteristics)

Based on Quranic evidence, the المجرمين are those who:

| Action | Arabic Term | Evidence |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Deny Allah's signs** | كَذَّبُوا بِآيَاتِنَا | 7:40, 10:17, 55:43 |
| **Are arrogant toward guidance** | استكبروا عنها | 7:40 |
| **Fabricate lies against Allah** | افترى على الله كذبا | 10:17 |
| **Turn away after being reminded** | ذُكِّرَ بِآيَاتِ رَبِّهِ ثُمَّ أَعْرَضَ عَنْهَا | 32:22 |
| **Commit injustice/wrongdoing** | ظلموا | 10:13 |
| **Mock the believers** | يضحكون / يتغامزون | 83:29-32 |
| **Deny the Day of Judgment** | يُكَذِّبُ بِهَا (Hell) | 55:43 |
| **Hate the truth** | كرهوا الحق | 8:8, 10:82 |
| **Plot against truth** | يمكرون | 6:123 |

### Note on 6:123 - The Leaders of the Mujrimeen

A critical verse reveals the **societal structure** of إجرام:

> وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلْنَا فِي كُلِّ قَرْيَةٍ **أَكَابِرَ مُجْرِمِيهَا** لِيَمْكُرُوا فِيهَا

"And thus We placed in every city **the greatest of its criminals** to plot therein"

This shows that:
1. Every city/community has مجرمين
2. Among them are أَكَابِر (the greatest/leaders) who plot (يمكرون)
3. Their plotting is against the message of truth
4. Yet they only plot against themselves (وَمَا يَمْكُرُونَ إِلَّا بِأَنفُسِهِمْ)

### 4. The Quranic Antonym: المحسنين

The Quran consistently contrasts المجرمين with المحسنين (those who do ihsan/excellence):

| Pattern | المجرمين | المحسنين |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Recompense formula | كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِي المجرمين | كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِي المحسنين |
| Examples | 7:40, 10:13, 46:25 | 12:22, 28:14, 37:80, 37:110 |
| Divine relationship | Enemies of prophets (25:31) | Allah is WITH them (29:69) |
| Outcome | لا يفلح (will not succeed) | Reward not lost (11:115, 12:56) |

### 5. Overlapping Terms

The Quran uses these terms interchangeably for the same actions:
- **المجرمون** - 10:17: "لا يفلح المجرمون"
- **الظالمون** - 6:21: "لا يفلح الظالمون" (same context: fabricating lies/denying signs)
- **الكافرون** - 23:117: "لا يفلح الكافرون"

This suggests these are not separate categories but overlapping descriptions of the same fundamental spiritual state.

## Traditional vs. Quranic Interpretation

| Aspect | Traditional Understanding | Quranic Evidence |
|--------|---------------------------|------------------|
| **Definition** | Criminals, sinners, evildoers (general) | Specifically: those who cut themselves off from truth after it comes to them |
| **Primary action** | Committing sins/crimes | Rejecting divine guidance + arrogance |
| **Core characteristic** | Doing bad deeds | Denial (تكذيب) + arrogance (استكبار) toward signs |
| **Scope** | Broad category of wrongdoers | Those who received truth and actively rejected it |

### What the Quran Adds

1. **إجرام is fundamentally about cutting off from truth** - The original meaning of the root (cutting/severing) is reflected in the Quranic usage: the مجرم is one who has severed themselves from guidance.

2. **It requires receipt of guidance first** - Verses like 32:22 show that إجرام happens when one is reminded (ذُكِّرَ) THEN turns away (أعرض). It is not mere ignorance.

3. **Arrogance is central** - The combination of denial (تكذيب) AND arrogance (استكبار) appears repeatedly. The extended search revealed a CRITICAL pattern in indefinite forms:

   **The Pattern فَاسْتَكْبَرُوا وَكَانُوا قَوْمًا مُّجْرِمِينَ appears in:**
   - 7:133 (Pharaoh's people after signs)
   - 10:75 (Pharaoh's people when Moses came)
   - 45:31 (Direct address: "you were arrogant and were a criminal people")

   This shows that استكبار (arrogance) is the GATEWAY to إجرام - first comes arrogance, then the state of being مجرم is confirmed.

4. **Social behavior matters** - Mocking believers (83:29-32) is explicitly identified as what the أجرموا did.

5. **The verb يجرمنكم reveals causation** - In 5:2, 5:8, and 11:89, the إجرام is specifically:
   - Transgressing limits (تعتدوا)
   - Being unjust (ألا تعدلوا)
   - Bringing destruction upon oneself

6. **11:35 reveals إجرام = افتراء على الله (fabricating about Allah)** ⭐ NEW
   > قُلْ إِنِ افْتَرَيْتُهُ فَعَلَيَّ **إِجْرَامِي** وَأَنَا بَرِيءٌ مِّمَّا **تُجْرِمُونَ**

   The Prophet is commanded to say: "If I have fabricated it, then upon me is my إجرام, and I am innocent of what إجرام you commit."

   This directly equates إجرام with افتراء (fabrication) - confirming that the core action of the مجرم is fabricating lies about Allah's message.

## Conclusion: The Quranic Definition of مُجْرِم

A **مُجْرِم** is not simply a "criminal" or "sinner" in the generic sense. The Quran defines the مجرم as one who:

1. **Receives clear guidance** (آيات، بينات، ذكر)
2. **Rejects it with arrogance** (تكذيب + استكبار)
3. **Turns away from it deliberately** (إعراض)
4. **Opposes those who accept it** (عداوة للأنبياء، استهزاء بالمؤمنين)
5. **Thereby severs themselves** from the path of righteousness

The antonym (المحسن) confirms this: the محسن is one who does إحسان (excellence in responding to guidance), while the مجرم is one who does إجرام (severing from guidance).

## Key Verse Summary

**The Path Distinction (6:55)**:
> وَكَذَٰلِكَ نُفَصِّلُ الْآيَاتِ وَلِتَسْتَبِينَ سَبِيلُ الْمُجْرِمِينَ
> "And thus do We detail the signs so that the way of the criminals becomes clear"

This verse indicates that Allah details the signs precisely SO THAT the سبيل (path/way) of the مجرمين becomes distinct and recognizable - showing that مجرمين follow a specific, identifiable path of rejection.
