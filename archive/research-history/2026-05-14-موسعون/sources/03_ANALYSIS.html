2. Analysis of Domain A: Divine Encompassing

Pattern: وَاسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ (7 occurrences)

The phrase وَٱللَّهُ وَٰسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ appears in: 2:115, 2:247, 2:261, 2:268, 3:73, 5:54, 24:32.

Observation: This is the most consistent pairing in the Quran for this root. وَاسِعٌ (Vast/Encompassing) is almost always paired with عَلِيمٌ (All-Knowing). The verses suggest that Allah's vastness manifests through His knowledge — His knowledge encompasses everything, just as His being encompasses all.

Contextual pattern: This phrase appears after mentions of:

  • Allah's provision and generosity (2:261, 2:268, 3:73, 5:54, 24:32)
  • Allah's sovereignty over all directions (2:115)
  • Allah's choice in granting authority (2:247)

This suggests وَاسِعٌ refers to Allah's unlimited capacity to give — His resources and knowledge have no bounds.

Pattern: وَسِعَ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ عِلْمًا (3 occurrences)

6:80, 7:89, 20:98 — "He has encompassed all things in knowledge."

Observation: The phrase uses the tamyīz construction (عِلْمًا as specification). His وسع specifically operates through عِلْم. This is consistent with the وَاسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ pairing above.

Pattern: وَسِعَتْ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ (2 occurrences)

7:156 — "My mercy has encompassed all things" (رَحْمَتِى وَسِعَتْ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ)

Observation: Here the subjects of وسع are رحمة (mercy) and عِلْم (knowledge). Both "encompass all things." This reveals a Quranic definition: Allah's vastness operates through His mercy and knowledge simultaneously.

Structural Analysis of 51:47-48

The verse pairs two cosmic realities:

  • Heaven: بَنَيْنَـٰهَا (We built it) + لَمُوسِعُونَ (We are expanding it) — past construction + ongoing expansion
  • Earth: فَرَشْنَـٰهَا (We spread it) — completed action

The shift from past tense (بَنَيْنَـٰهَا) to active participle (لَمُوسِعُونَ) within the same verse is grammatically significant. The construction happened in the past; the expansion is still happening.

5. The Expanding Universe — Scientific Alignment

What Science Says

  • 1929: Edwin Hubble observes galaxies moving away from each other — "redshift" proportional to distance. The universe is not static.
  • 1931: Georges Lemaître proposes the "primeval atom" hypothesis (later called the Big Bang) — the universe expanded from a dense state.
  • 1965: Arno Penzias and Robert Wilson discover Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) radiation — the afterglow of the Big Bang, confirming expansion.
  • 1998: Saul Perlmutter, Brian Schmidt, and Adam Riess discover the expansion is accelerating — driven by dark energy.
  • Today: The expansion rate (Hubble constant) is approximately 67-74 km/s/Mpc. Space itself is stretching.

What the Quran Says

وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ

The Quran's statement uses precisely the morphological tools to express ongoing, active expansion:

  1. Form IV (أَفْعَلَ / مُفْعِل): The causative form — "We are making it expand," not "it is vast" (which would be وَاسِعَة).
  2. Active Participle: Indicates continuous/habitual action — not a one-time event but an ongoing process.
  3. Emphatic construction (إِنَّا لَـ): Strengthens the certainty of this claim.

Intellectual Honesty: Limitations

The following caveats must be acknowledged:

  • Classical Arabic scholars (pre-20th century) did not interpret لَمُوسِعُونَ as cosmic expansion. Their interpretations (ability, provision, vastness) were linguistically valid.
  • The Quran is not a science textbook — its primary purpose is guidance, not cosmological description.
  • Retroactive scientific interpretation (i.e., reading modern science into ancient texts) carries the risk of confirmation bias.
  • However, the morphological precision — choosing Form IV causative participle specifically for the heaven, while using Form I stative everywhere else — is a textual fact, not an interpretive projection.
