# من هم الذين يسارعون في الخيرات؟
## Quranic Research: Who Are Those Who Hasten in Good Deeds?

---

## 1. Root Analysis (تحليل الجذور)

### Root س ر ع (S-R-')
**Basic meaning**: Speed, hastening, quickness

**يُسَارِعُونَ** is from Form III (فَاعَلَ → يُفَاعِلُ), pattern **مُفَاعَلَة**:
- This form indicates **mutual action or effort**, suggesting not just speed but a **competitive hastening** - racing with oneself and others toward good
- The pattern implies intensity and continuous effort

### Root خ ي ر (Kh-Y-R)
**Basic meaning**: Good, goodness, choice, preference

**الخَيْرَات** (al-khayrāt) - plural of خَيْر:
- Encompasses all forms of goodness: righteous deeds, acts of worship, charitable works, moral excellence

---

## 2. The Exact Phrase in the Quran (الآيات القرآنية)

The phrase **يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْخَيْرَاتِ** appears in **three locations**:

### Location 1: Surah Al-Anbiya 21:90 (الأنبياء)

**Context**: Describing the Prophets Zakariyya, Yahya, and their family

> وَزَكَرِيَّآ إِذْ نَادَىٰ رَبَّهُۥ رَبِّ لَا تَذَرْنِى فَرْدًا وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ ٱلْوَٰرِثِينَ (89)
> فَٱسْتَجَبْنَا لَهُۥ وَوَهَبْنَا لَهُۥ يَحْيَىٰ وَأَصْلَحْنَا لَهُۥ زَوْجَهُۥٓ ۚ **إِنَّهُمْ كَانُوا۟ يُسَـٰرِعُونَ فِى ٱلْخَيْرَٰتِ** وَيَدْعُونَنَا رَغَبًا وَرَهَبًا ۖ وَكَانُوا۟ لَنَا خَـٰشِعِينَ (90)

**Who are they?** The **Prophets and their righteous families** - specifically Zakariyya, Yahya, and their household

**Their characteristics** (from the verse):
1. يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْخَيْرَاتِ - They hasten in good deeds
2. يَدْعُونَنَا رَغَبًا وَرَهَبًا - They call upon Allah with hope (رغبا) and fear (رهبا)
3. كَانُوا لَنَا خَاشِعِينَ - They were humbly submissive to Allah

---

### Location 2: Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:57-61 (المؤمنون)

**Context**: Describing the believers who will inherit Paradise

> إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ هُم مِّنْ خَشْيَةِ رَبِّهِم مُّشْفِقُونَ (57)
> وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِـَٔايَـٰتِ رَبِّهِمْ يُؤْمِنُونَ (58)
> وَٱلَّذِينَ هُم بِرَبِّهِمْ لَا يُشْرِكُونَ (59)
> وَٱلَّذِينَ يُؤْتُونَ مَآ ءَاتَوا۟ وَّقُلُوبُهُمْ وَجِلَةٌ أَنَّهُمْ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمْ رَٰجِعُونَ (60)
> **أُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ يُسَـٰرِعُونَ فِى ٱلْخَيْرَٰتِ وَهُمْ لَهَا سَـٰبِقُونَ** (61)

**Who are they?** The **true believers** described by five characteristics:

1. **مِّنْ خَشْيَةِ رَبِّهِم مُّشْفِقُونَ** - From fear of their Lord, they are apprehensive
2. **بِآيَاتِ رَبِّهِمْ يُؤْمِنُونَ** - They believe in the signs of their Lord
3. **بِرَبِّهِمْ لَا يُشْرِكُونَ** - They do not associate partners with their Lord
4. **يُؤْتُونَ مَا آتَوْا وَقُلُوبُهُمْ وَجِلَةٌ** - They give what they give while their hearts are fearful
5. **إِلَى رَبِّهِمْ رَاجِعُونَ** - [Knowing] they will return to their Lord

**Additional description**: وَهُمْ لَهَا سَابِقُونَ - "and they are forerunners to it [good deeds]"

---

### Location 3: Surah Aal-Imran 3:113-114 (آل عمران)

**Context**: Describing righteous People of the Book who accepted Islam

> لَيْسُوا۟ سَوَآءً ۗ مِّنْ أَهْلِ ٱلْكِتَـٰبِ أُمَّةٌ قَآئِمَةٌ يَتْلُونَ ءَايَـٰتِ ٱللَّهِ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيْلِ وَهُمْ يَسْجُدُونَ (113)
> يُؤْمِنُونَ بِٱللَّهِ وَٱلْيَوْمِ ٱلْـَٔاخِرِ وَيَأْمُرُونَ بِٱلْمَعْرُوفِ وَيَنْهَوْنَ عَنِ ٱلْمُنكَرِ **وَيُسَـٰرِعُونَ فِى ٱلْخَيْرَٰتِ** وَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ مِنَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحِينَ (114)

**Who are they?** The **righteous among the People of the Book** (أُمَّةٌ قَائِمَةٌ)

**Their characteristics**:
1. **يَتْلُونَ آيَاتِ اللَّهِ آنَاءَ اللَّيْلِ** - They recite Allah's verses during the night
2. **وَهُمْ يَسْجُدُونَ** - And they prostrate (in prayer)
3. **يُؤْمِنُونَ بِاللَّهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ** - They believe in Allah and the Last Day
4. **يَأْمُرُونَ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ** - They enjoin good
5. **يَنْهَوْنَ عَنِ الْمُنكَرِ** - They forbid evil
6. **يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْخَيْرَاتِ** - They hasten in good deeds

**Result**: وَأُولَٰئِكَ مِنَ الصَّالِحِينَ - "Those are among the righteous"

## 3. Contrastive Usage (المقارنة القرآنية)

The Quran uses the same verb يُسَارِعُونَ with different objects to create a stark contrast:

### يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْخَيْرَاتِ (Hastening in Good)
- Prophets and their families (21:90)
- True believers (23:61)
- Righteous People of the Book (3:114)

### يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْكُفْرِ (Hastening in Disbelief)
**5:41**: يَا أَيُّهَا الرَّسُولُ لَا يَحْزُنكَ الَّذِينَ **يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْكُفْرِ**
**3:176**: وَلَا يَحْزُنكَ الَّذِينَ **يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْكُفْرِ**

### يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْإِثْمِ وَالْعُدْوَانِ (Hastening in Sin and Aggression)
**5:62**: وَتَرَى كَثِيرًا مِنْهُمْ **يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْإِثْمِ وَالْعُدْوَانِ**

## 5. Key Insights (الدروس المستفادة)

### The Linguistic Precision
The use of **يُسَارِعُونَ** (Form III) rather than **يُسْرِعُونَ** (Form IV) conveys:
- **Competitive haste** - racing toward good as if in a competition
- **Continuous effort** - not a one-time act but a consistent pattern
- **Intensity** - giving one's utmost in pursuing good

### The Three Categories of People Described:
1. **الأنبياء وأهلهم** - The Prophets and their families (highest example)
2. **المؤمنون** - The true believers among this Ummah
3. **الصالحون من أهل الكتاب** - The righteous among previous scriptures

### The Balance of Hope and Fear:
The phrase **رَغَبًا وَرَهَبًا** (21:90) reveals that true hastening in good combines:
- **رغبا** (رجاء/hope) - longing for Allah's reward
- **رهبا** (خوف/fear) - fear of Allah's displeasure

### The Heart's State:
Those who hasten in good do so with **قُلُوبُهُمْ وَجِلَةٌ** (23:60) - hearts trembling with fear, worried that their deeds might not be accepted.

---

## 6. Conclusion (الخلاصة)

The Quran identifies **يُسَارِعُونَ فِي الْخَيْرَاتِ** as a distinguishing quality of:

1. **The Prophets** - As role models who combined worship, supplication, and humility
2. **True Believers** - Those who perform good deeds while fearing they may not be sufficient
3. **Righteous Converts** - People of previous scriptures who embraced truth

The common thread is that hastening in good is inseparable from:
- Deep faith (إيمان)
- Pure monotheism (توحيد)
- Reverent fear of Allah (خشية)
- Hope combined with fear (رغبا ورهبا)
- Active worship and good works
- A heart that remains humble despite abundant deeds

This is not mere speed, but a **quality of the heart** that manifests in action - the believer races toward good while remaining humble, knowing their deeds depend entirely on Allah's acceptance.
