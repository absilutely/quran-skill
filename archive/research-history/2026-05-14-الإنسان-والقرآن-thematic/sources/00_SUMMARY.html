Critical Qur'anic Principles Identified

  1. Reciprocal Relationship: God revealed the Qur'an for humans; humans are expected to engage with it actively.
  2. Heart as Locus: The قلب (heart) is the primary organ of Qur'anic reception - sealed hearts cannot benefit. See Cardiac Domain.
  3. Gradational Engagement: The Qur'an presents progressive levels from hearing to acting. See Engagement Sequence.
  4. Universal Address, Conditional Benefit: The Qur'an addresses all humanity but its full benefits accrue to those who believe and engage properly.
  5. The Abandonment Warning: Neglecting the Qur'an (الهجر) is presented as a grave transgression. See Abandonment Verses.
