02 — Complete Verse Collection
الآيات: الدعاء والعبادة

All Quranic verses demonstrating the relationship between duʿāʾ (root د ع و) and ʿibādah (worship), organized by pattern category. Total: 26 key verses across 4 categories.

Category A: Direct Equation — Duʿāʾ Explicitly Equals ʿIbādah

The Quran directly equates duʿāʾ with ʿibādah within the same verse.

وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ عَنْ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدْخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ
غافر Ghāfir 40:60
"And your Lord said: Call upon Me; I will respond to you. Indeed, those who are too arrogant for My worship will enter Hell humiliated."
The pivotal verse. Command to make duʿāʾ (ادعوني) followed by labeling refusal as arrogance toward ʿibādah (عبادتي). The two acts are treated as one.

Category B: Proximity Pattern — Duʿāʾ Near Worship Commands

Verses where duʿāʾ commands appear in immediate textual proximity to ʿibādah commands or worship contexts.

B.1 — The Al-Aʿrāf Sequence (7:54–56)

إِنَّ رَبَّكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ فِي سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍ ثُمَّ ٱسْتَوَىٰ عَلَى ٱلْعَرْشِ
الأعراف Al-Aʿrāf 7:54
"Indeed, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and earth in six periods and then established Himself above the Throne."
Establishes Allah's lordship — the foundation for the duʿāʾ commands that immediately follow.
ٱدْعُوا رَبَّكُمْ تَضَرُّعًا وَخُفْيَةً ۚ إِنَّهُ لَا يُحِبُّ ٱلْمُعْتَدِينَ
الأعراف Al-Aʿrāf 7:55
"Call upon your Lord humbly and privately. Indeed, He does not like transgressors."
Duʿāʾ command positioned directly after lordship declaration — supplication as the natural response to recognizing Allah's sovereignty.
وَلَا تُفْسِدُوا فِي ٱلْأَرْضِ بَعْدَ إِصْلَاحِهَا وَٱدْعُوهُ خَوْفًا وَطَمَعًا ۚ إِنَّ رَحْمَتَ ٱللَّهِ قَرِيبٌ مِّنَ ٱلْمُحْسِنِينَ
الأعراف Al-Aʿrāf 7:56
"And do not cause corruption upon the earth after its reformation. And call upon Him in fear and hope. Indeed, the mercy of Allah is near to the doers of good."
Second duʿāʾ command reinforcing the first, specifying the spiritual states (خوفًا وطمعًا) that accompany it — identical to the states described for worshippers in 32:16.

B.2 — The Al-Baqarah Context (2:186)

وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ ٱلدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ
البقرة Al-Baqarah 2:186
"And when My servants ask you concerning Me, indeed I am near. I respond to the call of the caller when he calls upon Me."
Contains THREE forms from root د ع و (دعوة، الداع، دعان) alongside عبادي (My servants). Placed within the Ramadan fasting passage (2:183–187), situating duʿāʾ within a major ʿibādah context.

B.3 — The Ṭā-Hā Command (20:14)

إِنَّنِي أَنَا ٱللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنَا فَٱعْبُدْنِي وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ لِذِكْرِي
طه Ṭā-Hā 20:14
"Indeed, I am Allah. There is no deity except Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance."
Pairs the ʿibādah command (فاعبدني) with establishment of prayer (which is itself a form of duʿāʾ and dhikr), reinforcing the worship-supplication nexus.

B.4 — The Surah Ghāfir Cluster (40:14, 40:60, 40:65–66)

فَٱدْعُوا ٱللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ ٱلْكَٰفِرُونَ
غافر Ghāfir 40:14
"So call upon Allah with sincerity of religion for Him, even though the disbelievers dislike it."
Duʿāʾ qualified with ikhlāṣ (sincerity) — the same quality that defines true worship.
هُوَ ٱلْحَيُّ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ فَٱدْعُوهُ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ
غافر Ghāfir 40:65
"He is the Ever-Living; there is no deity except Him, so call upon Him with sincerity of religion."
The tawḥīd declaration (لا إله إلا هو) is immediately followed by a duʿāʾ command — supplication as the practical expression of monotheism.
قُلْ إِنِّي نُهِيتُ أَنْ أَعْبُدَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ
غافر Ghāfir 40:66
"Say: I have been forbidden to worship those you call upon besides Allah."
In the very next verse, ʿibādah (أعبد) and duʿāʾ (تدعون) are used interchangeably: worshipping what they call upon is the same act.

Category C: Interchangeability — Duʿāʾ Used as Synonym for ʿIbādah

C.1 — Paired Prohibitions

قُلْ إِنِّي نُهِيتُ أَنْ أَعْبُدَ ٱلَّذِينَ تَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ
الأنعام Al-Anʿām 6:56
"Say: I have been forbidden to worship those you call upon besides Allah."
Identical structure to 40:66. The Prophet is forbidden from worshipping (أعبد) what the polytheists call upon (تدعون) — proving the two acts are one.
إِن يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِهِ إِلَّا إِنَٰثًا وَإِن يَدْعُونَ إِلَّا شَيْطَٰنًا مَّرِيدًا
النساء An-Nisāʾ 4:117
"They call upon instead of Him none but female [deities], and they call upon none but a rebellious Satan."
يدعون (they call upon) describes the act of worshipping false deities — duʿāʾ as the defining act of devotion.

C.2 — Challenge Verses

وَٱلَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ لَا يَخْلُقُونَ شَيْئًا وَهُمْ يُخْلَقُونَ
النحل An-Naḥl 16:20
"And those they call upon besides Allah create nothing, and they themselves are created."
قُلِ ٱدْعُوا ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمْتُم مِّن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ ۖ لَا يَمْلِكُونَ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَلَا فِي ٱلْأَرْضِ
سبأ Sabaʾ 34:22
"Say: Call upon those you claim besides Allah. They do not possess an atom's weight in the heavens or on the earth."
The imperative ادعوا is used as a challenge: if calling upon them yields nothing, worshipping them is equally futile.
قُلِ ٱدْعُوا ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمْتُم مِّن دُونِهِۦ فَلَا يَمْلِكُونَ كَشْفَ ٱلضُّرِّ عَنكُمْ وَلَا تَحْوِيلًا
الإسراء Al-Isrāʾ 17:56
"Say: Call upon those you claimed besides Him, for they do not possess the removal of adversity from you nor its transfer."
وَيَوْمَ يَقُولُ نَادُوا شُرَكَائِيَ ٱلَّذِينَ زَعَمْتُمْ فَدَعَوْهُمْ فَلَمْ يَسْتَجِيبُوا لَهُمْ
الكهف Al-Kahf 18:52
"And the Day He will say: Call My partners whom you claimed. And they will call them but they will not respond to them."

C.3 — The Distress Paradigm

بَلْ إِيَّاهُ تَدْعُونَ فَيَكْشِفُ مَا تَدْعُونَ إِلَيْهِ
الأنعام Al-Anʿām 6:41
"Rather, it is Him alone you call upon, and He removes that for which you called upon Him."
Uses تدعون twice — in crisis, the duʿāʾ humans naturally direct to Allah alone is the purest expression of worship (fiṭrah).
وَلَا تَسُبُّوا ٱلَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ فَيَسُبُّوا ٱللَّهَ عَدْوًا بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ
الأنعام Al-Anʿām 6:108
"And do not insult those they call upon besides Allah, lest they insult Allah in enmity without knowledge."

C.4 — Additional Interchangeability Verses

وَمَا ظَلَمْنَٰهُمْ وَلَٰكِن ظَلَمُوا أَنفُسَهُمْ ۖ فَمَا أَغْنَتْ عَنْهُمْ ءَالِهَتُهُمُ ٱلَّتِي يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ مِن شَيْءٍ
هود Hūd 11:101
"And We did not wrong them, but they wronged themselves. And their deities which they called upon besides Allah availed them nothing."
أَلَا إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَن فِي ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَمَن فِي ٱلْأَرْضِ ۗ وَمَا يَتَّبِعُ ٱلَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ شُرَكَاءَ
يونس Yūnus 10:66
"Unquestionably, to Allah belongs whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth. And those who invoke besides Allah do not follow partners."
إِنَّمَا تَعْبُدُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ أَوْثَٰنًا وَتَخْلُقُونَ إِفْكًا
العنكبوت Al-ʿAnkabūt 29:17
"You only worship besides Allah idols, and you produce a falsehood."
This verse uses تعبدون (you worship) in the exact same structural slot where other verses use تدعون (you call upon) — proving the interchangeability of the two roots.
ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلْحَقُّ وَأَنَّ مَا يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِهِ ٱلْبَٰطِلُ
لقمان Luqmān 31:30
"That is because Allah is the Truth, and that what they call upon besides Him is falsehood."
فَٱعْبُدُوا مَا شِئْتُم مِّن دُونِهِ
الزمر Az-Zumar 39:15
"Then worship what you will besides Him."
فاعبدوا (then worship) used in the same مِن دُونِه (besides Him) formula where other verses use فادعوا — another proof of interchangeability.

Category D: Prophetic Model — Duʿāʾ as Core Prophetic Worship

وَأَعْتَزِلُكُمْ وَمَا تَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ وَأَدْعُو رَبِّي
مريم Maryam 19:48
"[Ibrahim said:] And I will withdraw from you and from what you call upon besides Allah, and I will call upon my Lord."
Ibrahim contrasts false duʿāʾ (تدعون من دون الله) with true duʿāʾ (أدعو ربي) — encapsulating the entire tawḥīd-shirk dichotomy through the lens of supplication.
ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِي وَهَبَ لِي عَلَى ٱلْكِبَرِ إِسْمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسْحَٰقَ ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي لَسَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَاءِ
إبراهيم Ibrāhīm 14:39
"Praise to Allah, who has granted to me in old age Ismail and Ishaq. Indeed, my Lord is the Hearer of supplication."
The divine attribute سميع الدعاء (Hearer of supplication) links Allah's essential attribute of hearing with the act of duʿāʾ. Also found in 3:38.
هُنَالِكَ دَعَا زَكَرِيَّا رَبَّهُ ۖ قَالَ رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةً طَيِّبَةً ۖ إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَاءِ
آل عمران Āl ʿImrān 3:38
"At that point, Zakariyyā called upon his Lord, saying: My Lord, grant me from Yourself good offspring. Indeed, You are the Hearer of supplication."
Zakariyyā's duʿāʾ demonstrates the prophetic model of supplication as worship — direct, personal, expectant.
تَتَجَافَىٰ جُنُوبُهُمْ عَنِ ٱلْمَضَاجِعِ يَدْعُونَ رَبَّهُمْ خَوْفًا وَطَمَعًا
السجدة As-Sajdah 32:16
"Their sides forsake their beds; they call upon their Lord in fear and hope."
The description of night prayer (قيام الليل) uses duʿāʾ language (يدعون) rather than ṣalāh language — equating nocturnal worship with calling upon Allah.
