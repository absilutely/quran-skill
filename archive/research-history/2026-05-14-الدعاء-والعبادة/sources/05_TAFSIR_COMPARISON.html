1. Traditional Understanding

The equation of duʿāʾ with ʿibādah is well-established in traditional Islamic scholarship. The ḥadīth "الدعاء مخ العبادة" ("Duʿāʾ is the marrow of worship") — reported by al-Tirmidhī — has been the basis for this understanding for centuries.
