# Morphological Analysis: الصلاة / الصلوة

## Root Information

**Root Letters**: ص ل و (ṣ-l-w) / ص ل ي (ṣ-l-y)

**Note on Spelling**: In the Quran, this word appears in two orthographic forms:
- **الصلوة** (with و) - The Uthmani script spelling used in the Mushaf
- **الصلاة** (with ا) - The standard Arabic spelling

This is significant because the Quranic spelling preserves an older orthographic convention.

---

## Lexical Meaning (Classical Sources)

### From Lisan Al-Arab (لسان العرب):
The root ص ل و has multiple meanings:
1. **الصَّلا** - The middle of the back, the part where the two sides of the back meet at the spine
2. **صَلَّى** (Form II verb) - To pray, to bless, to invoke blessings upon
3. **الصَّلاة من الله: الرحمة** - Salah from Allah means mercy/blessing
4. **الصَّلاة من الملائكة: الاستغفار** - Salah from angels means seeking forgiveness
5. **الصَّلاة من المؤمنين: الدعاء** - Salah from believers means supplication
6. **صَلِيَ النار** (Form I) - To burn in fire, to be exposed to fire's heat

### From Al-Mufradat by Al-Raghib Al-Isfahani:
- **الصلاة** in origin refers to supplication (الدعاء)
- When attributed to Allah and His angels: blessing and mercy
- The ritual prayer is called صلاة because it involves دعاء (supplication)

## Critical Distinction: Two Different Roots

The research reveals that the Quran uses words from two related but distinct semantic fields:

### Root 1: ص ل و → صَلَّى (Form II) → الصَّلَاة
- **Meaning**: To pray, to bless, to invoke mercy/blessings
- **Context**: Always positive - worship, blessing, supplication
- **Forms**: الصلاة, يُصَلِّي, صَلُّوا, المصلين

### Root 2: ص ل ي → صَلِيَ (Form I) → صَلْي
- **Meaning**: To burn, to be exposed to fire
- **Context**: Always in context of punishment in fire
- **Forms**: يَصْلَى, تَصْلَى, اصْلَوْها, صِلِيًّا

This distinction is crucial for understanding the Quranic usage.
