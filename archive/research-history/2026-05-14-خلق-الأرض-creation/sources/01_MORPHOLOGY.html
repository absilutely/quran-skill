4. The Dual Form (يَوْمَيْن)

The word يَوْمَيْن (yawmayn) is the dual form of يوم (yawm - day).

In Arabic, the dual form explicitly means "exactly two" - there is no ambiguity.

This form appears twice in Surah Fussilat:

  • خَلَقَ الْأَرْضَ فِي يَوْمَيْنِ (41:9) - earth in two days
  • سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ فِي يَوْمَيْنِ (41:12) - seven heavens in two days
