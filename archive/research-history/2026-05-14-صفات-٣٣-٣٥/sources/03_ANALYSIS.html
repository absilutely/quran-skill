The Patience-Spending Chain (#5 + #7)

Found together in 13:22, 22:35, 28:54, and 3:17. The pattern suggests that true patience (صبر) naturally leads to spending (إنفاق), as both involve sacrifice of what one values — time and comfort for patience, wealth for spending.

The Obedience-Guarding Pair (#3 + #9)

Linked in 2:238 (guard prayers + stand obediently) and 4:34 (قانتات حافظات). This pairing suggests that devout obedience (قنوت) and guarding (حفظ) are two facets of the same discipline — one active, one protective.

The Truthfulness-Charity Link (#4 + #7)

Shared root ص د ق: Both الصادقين (#4, Form I) and المتصدقين (#7, Form V) derive from the same root. This is not coincidental — the Quran defines true charity (صدقة) as an expression of truthfulness (صدق). Form V تفعّل adds reflexivity: to make oneself truthful through giving.
