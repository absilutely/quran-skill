WHY المتصدقين and NOT المنفقين? This is perhaps the most significant morphological observation. Both roots exist in the Quran:
• ن ف ق (Form IV: أنفق / ينفقون) — to spend, general spending. ~20 occurrences of ينفقون alone.
• ص د ق (Form V: تصدّق / المتصدقين) — to give charity, rooted in truthfulness/sincerity.

The Quran chose المتصدقين, connecting charity to its root in صدق (truthfulness) — the SAME root as quality #4 (الصادقين). This suggests that true charity is an expression of inner truthfulness, not merely financial spending.

Additional evidence: The root ن ف ق also produces المنافقين (hypocrites) — the very opposite of the qualities in 33:35. The Quran may be deliberately avoiding this root to prevent any morphological association between the charitable and the hypocrites.

Structural Observations

1. The verse opens with two Form IV participles (المسلمين, المؤمنين), then shifts to seven Form I participles, with one Form V (المتصدقين) in position 7. This creates a pattern: the first two are foundational states of being (submission, faith), while the next eight are active qualities that manifest from those states.

2. All ten qualities use the active participle form — not past tense, not masdar. This indicates ongoing, continuous states — these are not one-time actions but enduring characteristics of the person.

3. Every quality is listed in masculine-feminine pairs (المسلمين والمسلمات, etc.), emphasizing equal applicability to both men and women — the only verse in the Quran with this exhaustive dual listing for all ten qualities.

4. Two qualities have objects/modifiers: #9 (الحافظين فروجهم) and #10 (الذاكرين الله كثيرا). These are the final two qualities before the reward, suggesting they may be culminating or encompassing qualities.

5. Shared root between #4 and #7: الصادقين and المتصدقين both derive from ص د ق. This is the ONLY shared root among all ten qualities, creating an etymological bridge between truthfulness and charity.
