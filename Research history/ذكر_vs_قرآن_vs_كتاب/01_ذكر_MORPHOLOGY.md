# Morphological Analysis: ذِكْر (Dhikr)

## Root Letters
**ذ ك ر** (dhal-kaf-ra)

## Lexical Meaning (من لسان العرب)
The root ذ ك ر carries two primary meanings:
1. **Remembrance/Mention** (التذكر والذكر باللسان): To bring something to mind, to mention, to speak of
2. **Male** (الذكر ضد الأنثى): The masculine gender, as opposed to female

The first meaning is the one relevant to this study when ذكر refers to revelation.

---

## Morphological Variations in the Quran

### 1. Verbal Noun (المصدر): ذِكْر
| Form | Pattern | Meaning | Quranic Usage |
|------|---------|---------|---------------|
| ذِكْر | فِعْل | Remembrance, mention, reminder | The primary noun form used for revelation |
| ذِكْرَى | فِعْلَى | A reminder (feminine form) | Used for the function/effect of reminding |
| ذِكْرًا | Indefinite accusative | A remembrance/mention | Used as object of verbs |

### 2. With Definite Article: الذِّكْر
| Form | Case | Occurrences | Key Verses |
|------|------|-------------|------------|
| الذِّكْرُ | Nominative | Referring to revelation | 15:6, 15:9, 38:1, 38:8, 54:25 |
| الذِّكْرَ | Accusative | Object of verbs | 16:44, 25:18, 36:11, 43:5, 68:51 |
| الذِّكْرِ | Genitive | After prepositions | 16:43, 21:7, 21:105, 25:29, 38:1 |

## Pattern Analysis: When ذِكْر Refers to Revelation

The Quran uses **الذِّكْر** (with definite article) specifically to refer to divine revelation in the following patterns:

### Pattern 1: نُزِّلَ عَلَيْهِ الذِّكْرُ (Revelation sent down upon him)
- **15:6**: وَقَالُوا يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِي نُزِّلَ عَلَيْهِ الذِّكْرُ
- **38:8**: أَءُنزِلَ عَلَيْهِ الذِّكْرُ مِن بَيْنِنَا
- **54:25**: أَءُلْقِيَ الذِّكْرُ عَلَيْهِ مِن بَيْنِنَا

### Pattern 2: إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ (We sent down the Dhikr)
- **15:9**: إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ

### Pattern 3: أَهْلَ الذِّكْرِ (People of the Dhikr)
- **16:43**: فَاسْأَلُوا أَهْلَ الذِّكْرِ إِن كُنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ
- **21:7**: فَاسْأَلُوا أَهْلَ الذِّكْرِ إِن كُنتُمْ لَا تَعْلَمُونَ

### Pattern 4: اتَّبَعَ الذِّكْرَ (Following the Dhikr)
- **36:11**: إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ اتَّبَعَ الذِّكْرَ وَخَشِيَ الرَّحْمَنَ بِالْغَيْبِ

### Pattern 5: الْقُرْآنِ ذِي الذِّكْرِ (The Quran possessing Dhikr)
- **38:1**: صٓ ۚ وَالْقُرْآنِ ذِي الذِّكْرِ

## Total Quranic Occurrences (ذ ك ر root)
- **الذِّكْر** (as revelation): ~19 occurrences
- **ذِكْرًا** (indefinite): 10 occurrences
- **ذِكْرَى**: 11 occurrences
- **ذِكْرِي** (My remembrance): 5 occurrences
- Verb forms (اذكروا، يذكر، etc.): 200+ occurrences total
- **الذَّكَر** (male): ~10 occurrences (different meaning)

---

## Key Observation
The root ذ ك ر is highly productive in the Quran. When referring to revelation, it emphasizes the **function** and **purpose** of scripture—to remind humans of what they have forgotten or neglected. The الذِّكْر is that which causes تذكر (reflection/remembrance).
