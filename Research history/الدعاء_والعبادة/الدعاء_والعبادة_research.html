1. Summary of Key Findings (الملخص)

الجذر د ع و — الدعاء وعلاقته بالعبادة في القرآن
Root د ع و — Duʿāʾ and Its Placement Relative to Worship Commands

📑 Key Findings Summary

Finding 1: Direct Equation (المعادلة المباشرة)

The Quran explicitly equates duʿāʾ with ʿibādah in verse 40:60. The command to supplicate (ادعوني) is immediately followed by labeling its refusal as arrogance toward ʿibādah (عبادتي). This is the foundational proof that duʿāʾ is not a subcategory of worship — it is worship.

وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ٱدْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ عَنْ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدْخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ
غافر 40:60
"And your Lord said: Call upon Me; I will respond to you. Indeed, those who are too arrogant for My worship will enter Hell humiliated."

Finding 2: Proximity Pattern (نمط القرب النصي)

Duʿāʾ commands are consistently placed in immediate textual proximity to worship commands — after lordship declarations (7:54–56), within the fasting passage (2:186), alongside tawḥīd affirmations (40:65), and paired with prayer commands (20:14). This creates an inextricable contextual association.

Finding 3: Interchangeability (التبادلية)

The Quran uses the roots د ع و and ع ب د interchangeably when condemning shirk. The formula "I am forbidden to worship (أعبد) what you call upon (تدعون) besides Allah" (6:56, 40:66) treats duʿāʾ directed to other than Allah as identical to ʿibādah directed to other than Allah.

Conclusion (الخلاصة)

Duʿāʾ is the marrow of ʿibādah (مخّ العبادة). The Quran's deliberate placement strategies ensure this is understood not as an external association but as an intrinsic identity — to call upon Allah is to worship Him, and to call upon other than Allah is to worship other than Him.

4. Detailed Analysis (التحليل)

03 — Detailed Analysis
تحليل الأنماط والعلاقات

1. Pattern Analysis: Three Placement Strategies

Pattern 1: Direct Equation (المعادلة المباشرة)

Definition: The Quran explicitly labels duʿāʾ as ʿibādah within the same verse, creating an unambiguous semantic identity.

Evidence: 40:60 — "Call upon Me (ادعوني)" → refusal = arrogance toward "My worship (عبادتي)"

Significance: This is not metaphor or proximity — it is direct predication. The Quran does not say duʿāʾ is like ʿibādah or part of ʿibādah. It says the refusal to perform duʿāʾ is the refusal of ʿibādah.

Pattern 2: Proximity Placement (القرب النصي)

Definition: Duʿāʾ commands are placed in immediate textual proximity to ʿibādah commands or worship-context passages.

Pattern 3: Interchangeability (التبادلية)

Definition: The roots د ع و and ع ب د are used in identical syntactic structures with identical meanings when condemning shirk.

ما يدعون من دونه الباطل (31:30)[same semantic content, different root]

Significance: If the Quran treats "calling upon X besides Allah" and "worshipping X besides Allah" as the same condemnation, then calling upon = worshipping. This means directing duʿāʾ to other than Allah is, by Quranic definition, shirk.

2. Parallel Structures

Duʿāʾ and Ikhlāṣ (Sincerity)

The Quran qualifies duʿāʾ with مخلصين له الدين (with sincerity of religion) in both 40:14 and 40:65. This is the same qualifier used for worship itself (e.g., 98:5: مخلصين له الدين), further equating the two acts.

Duʿāʾ and the States of Worship

Both duʿāʾ and worship are described with the paired states خوفًا وطمعًا (in fear and hope):

  • 7:56: وادعوه خوفًا وطمعًا — "call upon Him in fear and hope"
  • 32:16: يدعون ربهم خوفًا وطمعًا — "they call upon their Lord in fear and hope"
