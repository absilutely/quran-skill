Coupled Word

عَلِيمٌ (All-Knowing) — Constant Companion of وَاسِعٌ

Observation: وَاسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ appears as a pair in 7 out of 8 occurrences of وَاسِعٌ. The only exception is 53:32 where it's وَاسِعُ ٱلْمَغْفِرَةِ (vast in forgiveness).

Research Question: Why does the Quran consistently pair vastness with knowledge? Does عِلْم in the Quran have a connection to encompassing/containing? Is there a Quranic definition of عِلْم that relates to وسع?

Root to research: ع ل م

Priority: High — this is the strongest coupling in the dataset

Potential Synonym

بَسَطَ (to spread/extend) vs. وَسِعَ (to encompass/be vast)

Observation: The root ب س ط appears frequently in contexts of provision (يَبْسُطُ ٱلرِّزْقَ — "He extends provision") which overlaps with one interpretation of لَمُوسِعُونَ. However, the Quran uses different roots suggesting distinct meanings.

Research Question: What is the precise distinction between بسط and وسع when applied to provision? Why does the Quran use يَبْسُطُ ٱلرِّزْقَ (9 occurrences) but never وَسَّعَ ٱلرِّزْقَ?

Root to research: ب س ط

Priority: Medium — helps define boundaries of وسع

Antonym

ضَيِّق / ضيق (Constriction/Narrowness)

Observation: The Quran's use of earth as وَاسِعَة (4:97, 29:56, 39:10) implies that earth can also be perceived as ضيق (narrow) for those who are oppressed. The contrast between expansion and constriction is thematically central.

Research Question: How does the Quran use ض ي ق? Is it always the opposite of وسع? Are there contexts where they overlap?

Root to research: ض ي ق

Priority: Medium

Thematic Connection

السَّمَاء (The Heaven/Sky) — Quranic Cosmology

Observation: 51:47 uses السَّمَاء (singular) not السَّمَاوَات (plural). The Quran sometimes distinguishes between "the heaven" (السماء — the sky/universe as a whole) and "the heavens" (السموات — the seven heavens). Which is being expanded?

Research Question: How does the Quran distinguish singular سماء from plural سموات? Does the singular refer to the observable universe/space while the plural refers to the layered heavens?

Root to research: س م و

Priority: High — directly impacts the interpretation of 51:47

Causal Relation

أَيْد (Power/Strength) — The Means of Construction

Observation: In 51:47, بِأَيْي۟دٍ is from root أ ي د (not ي د ي). The same root appears in 38:17 (ذَا ٱلْأَيْدِ — describing David) and in the concept of تأييد (divine support). The Quran pairs the construction of heaven with أَيْد specifically.

Research Question: How does the Quran use أ ي د? What does "strength" mean in divine context — is it different from قُوَّة (another word for power)?

Root to research: أ ي د

Priority: Medium

Quranic Definition

رَحْمَة وَسِعَتْ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ — Does Mercy Define Vastness?

Observation: In 7:156, Allah's mercy "encompasses all things" (وَرَحْمَتِى وَسِعَتْ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ). And in 40:7, the angels say "You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge" (وَسِعْتَ كُلَّ شَىْءٍ رَّحْمَةً وَعِلْمًا). This suggests the Quran defines divine vastness through mercy and knowledge specifically.

Research Question: Is there a Quranic definition of الواسع (the Vast) as the one whose mercy and knowledge encompass all? How does this relate to رَحْمَن (from root ر ح م)?

Root to research: ر ح م

Priority: High

Thematic Connection

يَزِيدُ فِى ٱلْخَلْقِ مَا يَشَآءُ (35:1) — Ongoing Addition to Creation

Observation: Sūrat Fāṭir 35:1 states "He increases in creation what He wills." This concept of ongoing addition to creation parallels the idea of ongoing expansion in 51:47. Several tafsirs (including the Urdu commentary retrieved) connect these two verses.

Research Question: Does يَزِيدُ فِى ٱلْخَلْقِ relate to لَمُوسِعُونَ? Are there other verses about creation being an ongoing (not completed) process?

Verse to examine: 35:1

Priority: High — directly relates to the expansion theme
