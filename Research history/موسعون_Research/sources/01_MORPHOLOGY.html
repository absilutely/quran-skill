3. Detailed Form Analysis

Form I: وَسِعَ — يَسَعُ (wasiʿa — yasaʿu)

Pattern: فَعِلَ — يَفْعَلُ

Base meaning: To be wide, to encompass, to contain. Intransitive — describes a state of vastness.

Quranic usage: Always with Allah or His attributes as subject. Used transitively with an object ("encompassed everything in knowledge"). The subject is always divine.

Key phrase: وَسِعَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ عِلْمًا — "encompassed everything in knowledge" (6:80, 7:89, 20:98)

Form IV: أَوْسَعَ — يُوسِعُ (awsaʿa — yūsiʿu)

Pattern: أَفْعَلَ — يُفْعِلُ

Base meaning: Causative — to make wide, to expand, to cause expansion. Also: to become wealthy/capable.

Quranic usage: Appears only as active participle مُوسِعُونَ (plural) and مُوسِع (singular).

Critical distinction: Form I وَسِعَ = "to be vast" (state). Form IV أَوْسَعَ = "to expand / make vast" (action). The use of Form IV participle in 51:47 implies active, ongoing expansion — not merely a state of being wide.

4. Grammatical Notes

4.1 The Emphatic Construction in 51:47

The phrase وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ uses triple emphasis:

  1. إِنَّ — emphatic particle ("indeed")
  2. نَا — first person plural (royal "We" / divine plural of majesty)
  3. لَـ — emphatic لام (لام التوكيد) before the predicate

This triple emphasis makes the statement exceptionally strong — "and indeed, We are certainly expanding [it]."

4.2 The Active Participle as Continuous State

In Arabic grammar, the active participle (اسم الفاعل) can indicate:

  • An ongoing/continuous state (حال)
  • A characteristic attribute (صفة)
  • A future meaning (مستقبل)

In 51:47, the use of the participle rather than a past tense verb (أوسعنا) or a present verb (نوسع) suggests an enduring, continuous state of expansion — "We are [continuously/always] expanding."

4.3 The Implicit Object

In وَإِنَّا لَمُوسِعُونَ, the object is implied — the most natural referent is السَّمَاء (the heaven/sky) from the beginning of the verse: وَالسَّمَاءَ بَنَيْنَاهَا بِأَيْدٍ. Thus: "And the heaven, We built it with strength, and indeed We are expanding [it]."
