Traditional vs. Quranic Comparison

Translation Comparison for Key Verses

41:10 - The "Four Days" Verse

Saheeh International:

"And He placed on it [i.e., the earth] firmly set mountains over its surface, and He blessed it and determined therein its [creatures'] sustenance in four days without distinction - for [the information of] those who ask."

Abdel Haleem:

"He placed solid mountains on it, blessed it, measured out its varied provisions for all who seek them––all in four Days."

Maududi (Tafhim):

"(After creating the earth) He set up firm mountains on it, blessed it, and provided it with sustenance in proportion to the needs of all who seek (sustenance). All this was done in four days."

Note: Maududi adds "(After creating the earth)" which clarifies the cumulative nature.

Traditional Understanding vs. Quranic Analysis

Aspect Traditional Understanding Quranic Analysis
Day count calculation Some scholars debated whether 2+4+2 creates a contradiction The 4 days is cumulative (2+2), making the total exactly 6
Nature of "days" Often taken as divine periods without specific duration Quran explicitly states 1 day = 1000 years (22:47, 32:5)
Heaven's initial state Generally described as formless Specifically described as "smoke" (دخان) - a unique term
Creation sequence Earth first, then heavens Earth created → furnished → heavens from smoke → completed

What Quran-Only Analysis Reveals

Insights Not Emphasized in Traditional Sources:

  • Mathematical precision: The phrase "سواء للسائلين" resolves apparent contradictions
  • Verb precision: Different verbs (خلق, جعل, قضى) for different creative acts
  • The "smoke" description: Unique cosmological insight rarely emphasized
  • Flexible "day" concept: The Quran itself defines days differently in different contexts
