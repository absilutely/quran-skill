Research Discoveries

Key Discoveries from This Research

1. The "Smoke" (دُخَان) State of Heaven

The Quran's description of heaven's initial state as "smoke" (دُخَان) in 41:11 is unique and significant:

  • This is the ONLY verse describing the pre-formed state of the heavens
  • The word suggests a gaseous, nebulous, unformed state
  • Modern cosmology describes the early universe similarly

Further research: Study the word دُخَان and its usage across the Quran.

2. The Phrase "سَوَاءً لِّلسَّائِلِينَ"

This phrase in 41:10 is key to understanding the cumulative day count:

  • Literally: "equal for those who ask"
  • Indicates a complete, final answer to the question of duration
  • Resolves the apparent 2+4+2=8 contradiction

3. Verb Choice Precision

The Quran uses different verbs for different creation acts:

VerbActionImplication
خَلَقَCreating earthCreation from nothing
جَعَلَPlacing mountainsArranging/positioning
قَدَّرَDetermining sustenanceMeasuring/proportioning
بَارَكَBlessingEndowing with goodness
قَضَىCompleting heavensFinishing/decreeing
