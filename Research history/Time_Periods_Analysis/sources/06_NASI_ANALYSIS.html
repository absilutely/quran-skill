1. Historical Context: Pre-Islamic Calendar Manipulation

Before Islam, the Arabs used a lunisolar calendar that employed intercalation (النسيء) — the practice of adding extra months to align the lunar calendar with the solar year.

Why Did They Do This?

  • Trade: To keep trade seasons consistent with harvests
  • Pilgrimage: To maintain Hajj in comfortable weather
  • Sacred Months: To manipulate when fighting was forbidden
