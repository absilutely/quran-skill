## 5b. أَجْرَمْنَا (We Committed Crime - 1st Person Plural Past) ⭐ NEW

### 1 Occurrence

| # | Verse | Arabic Text |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | 34:25 | قُل لَّا تُسْأَلُونَ عَمَّا **أَجْرَمْنَا** وَلَا نُسْأَلُ عَمَّا تَعْمَلُونَ |

**Significance**: This is the ONLY occurrence of the 1st person plural past tense. The Prophet is commanded to say: "You will not be asked about what crime WE committed, and we will not be asked about what you do."

**Key Insight**: This is a rhetorical/argumentative form. The Prophet uses "أَجْرَمْنَا" (assuming hypothetically: "what we committed") to establish the principle that each is accountable for their own actions. This rhetorical use is similar to 11:35 where the Prophet hypothetically says "if I fabricated it, then upon me is my إجرام."

**Significance**: This verse contains TWO unique forms:
1. **إِجْرَامِي** (ijrāmī) = "my crime" - the verbal noun (masdar) with 1st person possessive
2. **تُجْرِمُونَ** (tujrimūn) = "you (all) commit crime" - 2nd person plural present tense

**Key Insight**: The Prophet is commanded to say: "If I have fabricated it, then upon me is my crime, and I am innocent of what crime you commit." This shows that إجرام is specifically about fabricating lies about Allah's message - connecting directly to the defining action of the مجرمين.

## New Insight from Extended Search

### The Pattern: اسْتَكْبَرُوا وَكَانُوا قَوْمًا مُّجْرِمِينَ

The newly discovered indefinite forms reveal a CRITICAL pattern:

| Verse | Arabic Pattern |
|-------|----------------|
| 7:133 | فَاسْتَكْبَرُوا وَكَانُوا قَوْمًا مُّجْرِمِينَ |
| 10:75 | فَاسْتَكْبَرُوا وَكَانُوا قَوْمًا مُّجْرِمِينَ |
| 45:31 | فَاسْتَكْبَرْتُمْ وَكُنتُمْ قَوْمًا مُّجْرِمِينَ |

This confirms that **arrogance (استكبار) and إجرام are inseparable**. The pattern shows:
1. First comes the arrogance (استكبروا)
2. Then the state of being مجرمين is confirmed

This supports the core thesis that المجرم is not simply a "criminal" but one who:
- Received guidance → rejected it → with arrogance → thereby becoming مجرم
