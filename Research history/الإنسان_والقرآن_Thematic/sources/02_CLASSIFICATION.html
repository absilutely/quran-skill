Domain 1: المعرفية (Cognitive Domain)

Definition

The intellectual engagement with the Qur'an through faculties of reasoning, understanding, reflection, and contemplation.

Key Qur'anic Terms

  • التدبر (Tadabbur) - Deep reflection/pondering
  • التفكر (Tafakkur) - Contemplation/thinking
  • العقل ('Aql) - Reasoning/intellect
  • الفقه (Fiqh) - Deep understanding
  • التذكر (Tadhakkur) - Mindful remembrance

Classification Criteria

A verse belongs to this domain if it: commands or describes intellectual engagement with the Qur'an, references cognitive faculties in relation to revelation, rebukes lack of intellectual engagement, or describes outcomes of proper intellectual engagement.
